•	The game isn’t actually a trivia game

•	Game Idea: card game where you can answer the card to move forward or decline the card to move backwards. To progress or regress, the player has to roll a dice after they make the decision and the number they roll is the number they have to move, whether forward or backwards.

•	There are EASY CARDS and DIFFICULT CARDS

  o	Easy: contains actions/questions that the average player may or may not decline.
    -	Arm wrestle with the person in last place
    -	Swap places with the player in last place
    
  o	Difficult: contains questions that the average player would most likely decline.
    -	Shout “EARLY WORM GETS THE BIRD”
    -	Reenact forbidden memes
    -	Drop down and give me 20! 
    
•	There is an EASY route and a DIFFICULT route
  o	Easy: The easy route is longer than the difficult route. The easy route contains some DIFFICULT CARDS, but most of them should be easy.
  o	Difficult: contains actions/questions that the player (unless brave/competitive) would most likely decline. This route only contains DIFFICULT CARDS. If you accept the card, you move forward according to the roll of 1 dice. If you decline the card, you move backwards according to the roll of 2 die.
  
•	There should be an option on screen for the player to accept or decline the card, and based on that they should move forwards or backwards respectively.
